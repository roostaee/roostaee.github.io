Meysam Roostaee, Ph.D.

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Engineering at the University of Mazandaran. My research lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing (NLP), multimodal data analysis, and intelligent systems. I design computational methods that enable machines to comprehend complex information, uncover hidden patterns, and reason across diverse, interconnected data sources. A core dimension of my work is building trustworthy AI that remains reliable, robust, and safe when deployed in real-world, dynamic environments.

My work spans rapidly evolving domains, such as social network analysis, health informatics, and software repository mining, where text, visual content, and structured contextual data offer complementary perspectives. Specifically, our group explores intelligence across rich, nonconventional modalities, including strong>medical reports and chest X-rays in clinical health informatics, source code repositories and changelogs in software intelligence, and real-time hydrological telemetry for environmental decision-making.

I am passionate about bridging foundational methodological advances with applied real-world challenges. I actively welcome collaborations with motivated students, researchers, and industry partners who share a vision for pushing the boundaries of dependable and impactful AI.
